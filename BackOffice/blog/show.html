UFC 310: Everything You Need to Know Before Fight Night

Jake Morrison April 5, 2026 4,821 views

Marcus Santos enters the octagon as a heavy favorite, but Kyle Jensen's unorthodox striking style could be the great equalizer on fight night. This preview breaks down everything you need to know before the main card begins.

Full Article Content

The biggest fight of the year is almost here, and the MMA world is buzzing. UFC 310: Night of Champions promises to deliver a night of elite competition from top to bottom, but all eyes will be on the main event as Marcus "The Lion" Santos defends his heavyweight championship against the surging Kyle "Ice Cold" Jensen.

The Main Event Breakdown

Santos (24-3-1) enters this fight off the back of three consecutive title defenses, each more dominant than the last. His combination of elite wrestling and heavy hands has made him virtually impossible to finish, and his chin has never been seriously tested. Oddsmakers have him installed as a -340 favorite.

But Jensen (19-2) has been a revelation. The Swedish knockout artist has won his last six fights inside the distance, and his unpredictable southpaw stance and long-range power have given every opponent trouble. His boxing coach, two-time world champion Pierre Moreau, believes this fight is his to win.

The Co-Main Event

Supporting the headliner is a stacked card including the welterweight clash between Thompson and Diaz, a rematch four years in the making. The lightweight contender fight between Oliveira and Tsarukyan is expected to steal the show with its grappling-heavy action.

Prediction Corner

Our analysts are split down the middle. Three favour Santos by late stoppage, citing his durability and championship pedigree. Two believe Jensen's power is the X-factor and that one clean right hand could end the night early. Make your predictions on SmartFight before the opening bell rings at T-Mobile Arena.

UFC 310 takes place Saturday, May 15, 2026 at T-Mobile Arena in Las Vegas, Nevada. Main card begins at 10:00 PM ET.
