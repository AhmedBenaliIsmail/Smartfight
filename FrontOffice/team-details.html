Biography

Marcus "The Lion" Santos is the reigning UFC Heavyweight Champion, known for his explosive power striking and elite wrestling credentials. Born in São Paulo, Brazil, Santos began training in mixed martial arts at age 15, earning his professional debut at 20.

Santos captured the heavyweight title at UFC 298 with a stunning second-round TKO, and has since made five successful defenses. His combination of raw power, technical precision, and relentless pressure makes him arguably the most complete heavyweight in the sport's history. He enters UFC 310 looking to cement his legacy as the greatest heavyweight of all time.
