FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

How do I change or cancel my ticket booking?

Log into your account, go to My Bookings, and select Manage. Cancellations must be made at least 48 hours before the event for a full refund.

When do fight card predictions close?

Predictions lock 30 minutes before the first bell of the event. Make sure you submit all your picks before the deadline.

How can I apply for press credentials?

Send your media outlet details and press pass request to press@smartfight.com. Allow 5 business days for a response.

Are VIP packages available for all events?

VIP packages are available for selected major events. Check the Bookings page for current availability and pricing for each event.
