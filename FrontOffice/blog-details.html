UFC 310: Santos vs Jensen — The Complete Breakdown of the Year's Biggest Fight

Jordan Kane
Senior Fight Analyst
April 5, 2026 8 min read 24.8K views

When the cage door closes at T-Mobile Arena on May 15th, Marcus "The Lion" Santos will step in as the reigning, defending heavyweight champion for the sixth time. Across the octagon will stand Kyle "Ice Cold" Jensen, a man who has spent two years watching film, drilling counters, and quietly building the kind of game plan that no champion wants to face.

This is not a routine title defense. Santos has finished all five of his previous challengers, but Jensen represents something different — a fighter who doesn't panic under pressure, doesn't get drawn into brawls, and has an answer for every tool in the champion's arsenal. This fight has the potential to be the most technically complex heavyweight title bout in recent memory.

The Champion's Path: What Santos Does Best

Marcus Santos is a machine built for one purpose: violence at close range. His jab is a weapon of destruction, setting up the overhand right that has ended 12 professional careers. But what separates Santos from other power punchers is his wrestling. With Division I credentials and a dozen submission wins, Santos can force fights anywhere and punish you on the ground or standing.

"People see the knockouts and think I'm a brawler. I'm not. I'm a chess player who happens to punch like a freight train." — Marcus Santos, pre-fight press conference

His most dangerous sequence is the jab-right-left hook combination that he throws off the clinch break. Jensen will need to anticipate this and be ready to either time the exit or clinch immediately after any failed exchange.

The Challenger's Blueprint: How Jensen Plans to Win

Jensen's strengths are subtle but lethal. He possesses an elite understanding of range, staying just outside Santos's power zone while landing the kind of crisp, accurate strikes that accumulate damage over five rounds. His chin is arguably the best in the division — he has been hit by some of the sport's biggest power punchers and walked through it.

The key to Jensen's game plan will be movement. Santos is dangerous when he can plant his feet and load up. Jensen's job is to never let him settle, to make him chase, to exhaust him mentally as much as physically. If he can drag this into a championship distance fight, his gas tank and cardio — both elite — could be decisive factors.

Key Matchups and X-Factors

Three key sub-matchups will determine the outcome of this fight. First: Santos's pressure vs Jensen's footwork. Second: Jensen's jab vs Santos's jab. And third — and most critically — the clinch. Both men are strong in the clinch but for different reasons. Santos looks for dirty boxing and takedowns; Jensen is a master of the arm drag and works constantly to get to the back.

If Jensen gets Santos's back even once in round one, the entire complexion of the fight shifts. Santos will become more hesitant, more defensive, and that defensive posture opens up his chin to Jensen's straight right.

Prediction

This is the closest heavyweight title fight in years, and anyone claiming they know what happens is fooling themselves. Santos's power gives him a first-round finish edge — one good punch and it's over. But if this goes past round two, Jensen's technical skill, movement, and cardio make him a serious threat to win a decision.

Our prediction: Jensen by decision in a fight of the year candidate. Santos will have his moments, but Jensen's discipline and chess-like approach will be too much to overcome over 25 minutes.
